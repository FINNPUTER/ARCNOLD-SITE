How to buy

Four sets. No spotter needed.

  1. Get a wallet

    Install an EVM wallet like MetaMask or Rabby and back up your seed phrase offline.

  2. Add Arc

    Add the Arc network to your wallet so it can see the chain.

  3. Load USDC

    Arc uses USDC for gas. Bridge or send some USDC to your wallet on Arc.

  4. Swap for $ARCNOLD

    Paste the contract address into a DEX on Arc and swap. The address shows up here on launch day.
